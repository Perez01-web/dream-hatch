# DREAMHATCH WEBSITE SPECIFICATION

### Version 1.1 — Website Foundation & Content Specification

**Organization:** DREAMHATCH
**Motto:** *Where Ideas Take Flight.*
**Location:** Blantyre, Malawi
**Website Type:** Organizational / Social Enterprise / Programs & Opportunities Platform
**Primary Audience:** Youth, women, students, entrepreneurs, job seekers, organizations, partners and the wider community.

---

# 1. Project Overview

DreamHatch needs a professional website that establishes it as a **Malawi-based social enterprise focused on creating economic opportunities and community impact**, rather than presenting it simply as a training organization.

The website will serve as DreamHatch's:

* Official online presence
* Organizational profile
* Program showcase
* Training and opportunity platform
* Registration/enquiry channel
* Communication and contact point
* Foundation for future projects, partnerships and community initiatives

The current **Intensive Computer (ICT) & Graphic Design Short Course** will be the first featured opportunity, but the architecture must allow future courses, programs, projects and events to be added without redesigning the website.

> **Design principle:** Build a website for DreamHatch as an organization, not a website for the August 2026 course. The course is the first opportunity flowing through the platform — not the definition of DreamHatch.

# 12. REGISTRATION / ENQUIRY (V1 Scope)

### Option A — WhatsApp

Prominent WhatsApp button, pre-filled message where possible.

General: *"Hello DreamHatch, I am interested in your training opportunities. I would like to know more about registration."*

Course-specific: *"Hello DreamHatch, I am interested in the ICT & Graphic Design Short Course. I would like to know more about registration."*

### Option B — Website Form

**Fields:** Full Name · Phone Number · Email (optional) · Program/Opportunity of Interest (dropdown: ICT & Graphic Design / Digital Skills / Entrepreneurship / Other / General Enquiry) · Message · Preferred Contact Method (Phone / WhatsApp / Email)

**Submit button:** Send Enquiry
**Confirmation:** "Thank you! Your enquiry has been received. DreamHatch will contact you shortly."

### Explicitly out of scope for V1

Online payments, user accounts/passwords, complex application management, course dashboards, automated admissions, payment verification.

> **Purpose:** Collect interest → notify DreamHatch → DreamHatch follows up. Nothing more, for now.

# 13. TRUST & CREDIBILITY RULES

The website must **never fabricate**: testimonials, statistics, partnerships, awards, accreditation, project results, founding history, team members, registration claims, or client logos. If DreamHatch hasn't provided it, it doesn't appear.

* **Certificate:** Always "Certificate of Completion." Never "Accredited Certificate" unless DreamHatch provides official recognition documentation.
* **"Registered social enterprise":** Safe to state as description. Before displaying a prominent "Registered" badge/credential, confirm the underlying registration documentation with DreamHatch first.

# APPENDIX A — Design & Technical Reference

*(Reference material for the design/build phase — not gating decisions that need re-approval before moving forward.)*

**Visual direction:** Respect the existing logo — black, white, lightbulb, circular typography, minimalist, "EST 2026," "WHERE IDEAS TAKE FLIGHT." Tone: professional + youthful + innovative + trustworthy. Avoid overly corporate, school-website, government-portal, overly colorful, or overly futuristic looks.

**Color:** No official brand colors beyond the logo. Use the black/white foundation and introduce one accent color for CTAs, links, highlights and active navigation — agree the final choice with DreamHatch rather than assuming it.

**Typography:** Prioritize readability, strong headings, clear hierarchy, short paragraphs. The motto can have a distinctive treatment; body text stays plain and readable.

**Imagery:** Use real DreamHatch photos wherever possible (training, youth activities, entrepreneurship, ICT/digital skills, community activities, women empowerment, team/staff) over stock photography.

**Mobile-first & responsiveness:** Design primarily for mobile — Call/WhatsApp/Register/Enquire must always be easy to reach, nav collapses to a mobile menu, buttons sized for touch. Site should also work properly on tablets, laptops and desktops with layouts designed per screen size, not just shrunk.

**Accessibility:** Sufficient contrast, meaningful headings, labeled buttons and forms, alt text on meaningful images, keyboard-accessible navigation, readable font sizes, visible focus states.

**Performance:** Given likely mobile-network access in Malawi — optimized images, minimal unnecessary JS, fast initial load, lazy-loaded images, compressed assets, simple animations, no unnecessary libraries. Beautiful but slow is not acceptable.

**SEO foundation:** Unique page titles, meta descriptions, proper heading structure, descriptive URLs, image alt text, Open Graph metadata, favicon. Natural (not stuffed) use of terms like "DreamHatch Malawi," "ICT training in Blantyre," "digital skills training Malawi," "entrepreneurship training Malawi," "youth/women empowerment Malawi."

**Footer:** DreamHatch name + motto + short description · Nav links · Contact details · Facebook · Register/Enquire CTA · © 2026 DreamHatch. All rights reserved.

**Future phases (not V1, just don't design against them):**
- *Phase 2:* News & Events, Gallery, Testimonials, Success Stories, Projects, Partners, Impact statistics, online applications.
- *Phase 3:* Admin dashboard, program/applicant management, registration database, automated notifications, online payments, user accounts, certificates, participant dashboards.

Introduce these only when DreamHatch has an actual operational need for them.
